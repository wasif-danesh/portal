This is the new home for my apps, articles and research. It replaces my older static site.

## What you'll find here

- **Apps** — the products I'm building, with notes on how they work.
- **Writing** — articles on machine learning, agents, cloud engineering and security.
- **About** — background, skills and how to reach me.

## How it's built

The site is a Next.js app with Tailwind CSS. Posts are plain MDX files in a `content/writing`
folder, validated at build time, so publishing is just adding a file and deploying.
